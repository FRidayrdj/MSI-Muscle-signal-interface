# 2026-11-24 — Day 2: power supply

Today I'm writing up the 3.3 V regulator and power supply stage, which isn't the flashy part of the amplifier, but nothing else works without it. I measured the rail at 3.294 V, about 6 mV under nominal, and wanted that number written down before I forget it. Every bias point in the amplifier hangs off this rail, so any noise on it shows up on the output.
